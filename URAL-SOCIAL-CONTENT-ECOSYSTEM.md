# URAL Travel — Master Social Content Ecosystem & Production Strategy

**Lifecycle: CURRENT PROPOSED PROGRAMME · Prepared 26 October 2026**\
**Evidence:** owner's corrected GSC tables, existing keyword research, the 51-article inventory, current audit and inspected content.\
**Execution state:** production drafts and a relative calendar; publication dates, source rechecks and live posts remain unrecorded.

## Recommendation

Run an **eight-week, existing-content-first programme**: **two Bengali consumer assets each week**, plus **one English LinkedIn post every second week**. This gives **16 distinct consumer decision assets and four professional posts**, supported by 16 Story and 16 WhatsApp adaptations.

The first programme needs **zero new website articles**. Its strongest starting points are payment preparation, Umrah eligibility, flight comparison and family logistics. The historically visible immigration article receives high update priority, but is held from promotion until its broad claims are corrected.

Eight weeks is derived from **16 useful consumer angles ÷ two a week**, with repeat appearances of a source spaced across different decisions. It is not inherited from the previous 21- or 30-day plans. The capacity assumption is one producer with approximately **4–6 hours a week**, to be checked against actual production time in the first two weeks.

## 2. What the evidence changes

### FACT

- The old endorsement page has **5 clicks / 270 impressions** and the Dhaka immigration article **2 / 137**, in the supplied historical pages.dev page table.
- The old flight hub has **0 / 70**, the Umrah hub **0 / 60**, the Saudi-flight article **0 / 10** and the DIY article **0 / 8**.
- The largest relevant historical query is `direct flights from dhaka`: **0 clicks / 25 impressions**. All 60 supplied historical query rows show zero clicks.
- New-domain pages have only **1–6 impressions each** in the supplied rows and zero reported clicks. Current query names have no supplied per-query counts.
- There are 51 existing article topics with EN/BN versions; they are not 102 separate topics to commission.

### INFERENCE

Payment and airport-preparation content have the best observed historical page-engagement evidence in this packet. Flights and Umrah have repeated visibility plus a substantial useful content base. This supports a connected Bangladesh-outbound programme, rather than restricting every post to a broad Umrah pitch or pivoting to the UK because the domain ends in `.co.uk`.

### RECOMMENDATION

Lead with the strongest usable existing sections. Give the airport article high **repair** priority. Use early Nepal, UAE and tool signals to preserve optional tests, not to promise current prices, visa access or new routes. Treat irrelevant queries and utility-page impressions as noise for this campaign.

The word **STRONG** in this package means relatively well-supported **priority within the supplied small evidence set**, not statistically proven demand, current ranking or market fit. No historical query is declared strong on one impression alone.

“Promote now” describes editorial suitability of the chosen angle. It is not a claim that a social asset is already designed, freshly source-checked, approved or scheduled. The asset register therefore starts at **DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK**.

The core programme draws from **11 existing articles and the airport-code tool**. Ten of those articles supply the consumer sequence; the affiliate-experiment article supplies a professional trust post. Reuse is selective and decision-led.

### Why several attractive sources are held

- **Immigration:** strong historical page evidence; universal document and clearance-speed claims need correction.
- **Cheap-flight and general baggage guides:** useful topics; Tuesday reset, fixed savings and blanket 46 kg/Zamzam claims prevent promotion.
- **Cash/card article:** useful decision guide; its client-side eSIM/OTP promise must be fixed first.
- **Hotel/Haramain guide:** useful checklist; accommodation-led promotion depends on an honest handoff rather than a Klook activities component.
- **Umrah worksheet:** useful action asset; the hub's sequence, summary and capability wording need focused review before a tool-led push.
- **Ziyarah:** five early Bengali impressions are a reason to retain it, not to repeat unsupported “no tariff exists” or settled-hours claims. Its source and connections need a bounded correction.
- **Dubai visa, passport and regional budgets:** meaningful questions, but current applicability, fee arithmetic or comparable cost bases must be verified before reuse.
- **Umrah–Dubai, fresh-passport ladder and Western/Saudi bridge:** timing, eligibility and savings overclaims keep these on HOLD.

Hajj remains a separate seasonal topic. A current Hajj query does not permit importing Umrah instructions or repeating an old registration deadline as current.

### Pacing and substitutions

Consumer posts start in **Tuesday/Friday 20:00 Asia/Dhaka** test slots, with companion Stories and next-day WhatsApp reuse. LinkedIn uses **Sunday 10:00** in alternate weeks. These are production starting assumptions, not measured audience peaks.

After two weeks, inspect actual reach, retention, qualified visits, useful questions and production hours. Change one timing/format variable at a time. A blocked source moves out of the slot; choose the next ready asset. If SOC-057 becomes verified, it can replace an unscheduled lower-priority asset such as SOC-012 or SOC-017; it does not add a third weekly consumer commitment. Update the calendar and retain both IDs.

The same source returns after several weeks with a different question. Its Story/WhatsApp derivatives serve channel-specific access, not a same-day flood of nominally new posts.

## 6. Platform and language roles

| Channel | Role and format | Language | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Plain-language explanation, concise checklist, native Reel/carousel | Bengali | Useful answer in-post; optional exact guide link |
| Instagram | Six Reels, concise cards, two-frame Stories | Bengali | Feed value first; a tested Story link route for deeper detail |
| WhatsApp | Short personal-use reminder; Status or opted-in audience | Bengali | Useful text that can stand alone; relevant guide link, no unsolicited broadcast |
| LinkedIn | Evidence, comparison method, travel-tech and operator questions | English | Professional discussion and relevant reference; no consumer-ad stream |
| Medium | One focused English analysis, held as an optional extension | English | Distinct adaptation with attribution; canonical check for substantial republication |
| Substack | One short English analytical dispatch, held as an optional extension | English | Real opt-in distribution and a useful source link; not a copy of the website |
| Reddit/community | Reply to an actual relevant question in its native context | Match the community/thread | HOLD until question and rules are known; no planned link dumping |

Use everyday Bengali with familiar travel terms. A professional English article about Bangladesh's banking system must keep its Bangladesh scope; an international itinerary analysis should not be artificially framed around Dhaka when it does not need to be.

Destination inspiration belongs only when it helps a decision—what a visit involves, whom it suits, how much time to allow on a verified basis. Empty skyline montages and motivational travel slogans add no useful reason to follow URAL.

## 7. Funnel and CTA logic

| Stage | Example | What counts as progress |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | SOC-007 airport-code reminder | Relevant reach, comprehension, useful question |
| Consideration | SOC-002, 003, 012, 017 | Saved comparison/clarification; deeper matched guide visit |
| Planning | Checklists, own-quote basis, assistance and account preparation | A real next-step question, useful guide consumption or a verified task action |
| Action | Existing guide's appropriate next step; worksheet reserve after its gate | Actual supported task completion, genuine partner exit or received enquiry |
| Trust | SOC-005, 010, 015, 020 | Substantive professional response, source engagement or relevant partnership conversation |

A post need not force a booking CTA. The core scripts use planning/guide actions; direct service, hotel-booking and worksheet-led pushes wait for their specific known blockers. Readers can still reach appropriate editorial and partner information through existing guides, but the campaign must not claim the downstream conversion path is already verified.

Examples of useful Bengali CTAs:

- “কার্ডের প্রস্তুতির ধাপগুলো পুরো গাইডে আছে।”
- “নিজের quote মিলিয়ে নিতে budget-check অংশ পড়ুন।”
- “Booking-এর airport name-এর সঙ্গে তিন অক্ষরের code মিলিয়ে নিন।”

Avoid generic “read our blog,” fake urgency, guaranteed cheapest, fixed savings and comment-keyword bait. If asking a question, ask one that could improve the next content decision, such as the confusing step or itinerary type—not for personal documents or engagement for its own sake.

## 8. Measurement and the learning loop

Use the [handbook's metric definitions](docs/03-social/PRODUCTION-HANDBOOK.md#6-measurement-definitions) and blank scorecard. Every result has an actual period, platform, asset ID and evidence location.

**Attention → useful visit → planning action → genuine enquiry/partner exit → confirmed outcome → next decision.**

Known measurement issues remain open: GTM forwarding, affiliate/internal click classification, WhatsApp share versus contact, incoming SubID propagation, and success messages without confirmed delivery. The content plan does not repair or certify those systems.

Track available platform metrics and manually verified received questions honestly while those gaps are resolved. Record unavailable downstream measures as **UNVERIFIED / NOT MEASURED**, not zero. A WhatsApp opening is a contact attempt; it is not a received enquiry. A click is not a booking. Approved commission is not payout.

| Observation | Next decision |
|---|---|
| Useful topic, weak early retention | Simplify the opening or visual explanation; retain the factual boundary |
| Saves/shares but few visits | Check whether the post already completes the task; strengthen the deeper reason to visit only when useful |
| Visits but no useful next step | Inspect the promised answer, mobile page and CTA destination |
| Planning questions repeat | Improve the owning article/section or make a new angle before proposing a new URL |
| Handoffs but no partner outcome | Check availability, attribution, time lag and checkout conditions before blaming content |
| Low numbers or broken measurement | Extend observation or repair measurement; do not declare the audience/niche a failure |

No numerical revenue target, required conversion rate, guaranteed impression threshold or automatic “cut after 30 visits” rule is justified by the supplied evidence. Compare like formats within the actual observation period and keep creator outcomes separate.

## 9. Phase B — after the existing campaign map

The [future-gap register](docs/03-social/FUTURE-CONTENT-GAPS.md) has a separate **GAP-001…** sequence. It distinguishes:

1. A social format missing from an existing article.
2. An existing article needing a correction or clearer answer.
3. A missing visual/evidence asset.
4. A genuinely unserved reader job that could justify a new article later.

The first three usually need no new URL. A matched-quote comparison needs real matched quotes, not an invented case study. Repeated Manali queries justify checking the intended journey, not announcing a flight or automatically writing another guide.

## 10. What the producer needs before the first publication

1. Choose the start week and confirm the actual 4–6-hour capacity assumption.
2. Read the first week's source packet; complete the source/landing checks for those two assets.
3. Make the specified graphics/video, proofread Bengali and test the real publishing account's link route.
4. Fill the calendar's publication date and review fields; schedule only ready assets.
5. Confirm the measurement fields that can genuinely be observed. Keep unavailable stages marked as such.

The desk's service scope, received-enquiry route, partner approval/attribution and private analytics remain unresolved inputs for a commercial-action push. This programme is usable as an editorial production system while keeping those claims and outcomes accurate.
