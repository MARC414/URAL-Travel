## What is not a content gap

- A missing Facebook carousel for an existing article is a **social-production task**.
- An incorrect title, CTA, source date or Bengali link is a **targeted source/implementation correction**.
- Generic hotel handoffs, unconfirmed desk services and false success states are **action reliability issues**. New prose does not solve them.
- Unknown GA4/partner outcomes are **measurement gaps**, not evidence that more articles are needed.
- A competitor covering more topics is not a reason to match its page count.
- Hajj/Ramadan needs the existing seasonal article rechecked against applicable notices before any proposal for another URL.
- Existing Dhaka–Kolkata coverage means the old “no India content” gap is obsolete.
