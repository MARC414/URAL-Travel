# Source packet and publication dependencies

**Prepared 26 October 2026.** The social drafts were checked against existing article sections, source-review records and the current audit. This is not a fresh certification of all travel regulations or supplier services. No real visa application, booking, permit slot or enquiry was submitted for this task.

## G-01 — normal pre-publication check

For the exact asset being produced:

1. Open its source article in the intended locale and read the specified section, summary and CTA. Compare it with the supplied draft.
2. For factual/volatile instructions, reopen the responsible primary source below. Record **checked date + applicable scope + any change** in the register/source trace. Recommendations such as asking for written terms must remain framed as advice.
3. If the primary page cannot be verified, keep only the supported general decision/process, or HOLD the affected assertion. A retrieval failure does not prove that a rule or price does not exist.
4. Test the exact landing URL and intended action. Check the post's preview, mobile page and locale. An unrelated or misleading destination blocks the linked asset.
5. Proofread Bengali, graphics and captions together. Put a qualification in the visual/spoken content when it materially changes the short claim.

If a newly found contradiction affects the post, change its status to NEEDS-UPDATE; do not simply shorten away the condition. A source date in metadata is not proof that every clause was rechecked on that date.
