- **Format / language:** REEL / bn

## SOC-020 — Accessibility is a chain of confirmations, not a neighbourhood label.

- **Format / language:** LINKEDIN TEXT / en
- **Source:** [Umrah with Elderly Parents from Bangladesh: Airline Wheelchair, Haram Mobility & Medical Preparation](https://uraltravel.co.uk/blog/umrah-with-elderly-parents-bangladesh-wheelchair-medical-guide) — Airline handover; exact property route; family planning boundaries
- **Decision / evidence:** Explain the operational detail that makes travel advice useful to families. (SF-13; EXPERIMENTAL at the stated scope.)
- **Status / gate:** DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK; G-01
- **Funnel / KPI:** TRUST; Relevant operator replies; partnership-quality conversations; qualified reference visits
- **Visual direction:** Text-only; optional schematic chain labelled questions to confirm. No certification badge or inspected-property photo.
- **Do not claim:** No actual partnership, property inspection, medical assessment or service guarantee.

### LinkedIn copy

Accessibility is a chain of confirmations, not a neighbourhood label.

For an older traveller, “near the mosque” leaves important questions unanswered. Which hotel entrance? Which lift? Which room and bathroom? What is the route to the entrance the family will actually use—and the way back?

The airline side has its own chain: the help requested, the operating carriers, any transit handover and the return point for a personal wheelchair.

A useful guide can turn these unknowns into specific questions. It should not turn a general area description into an access guarantee, or a published assistance policy into a confirmed booking.

URAL’s elderly-parent Umrah guide uses this question-led approach. It is not an inspected property list or a medical clearance.

For operators and properties, the practical opportunity is straightforward: answer the exact journey questions clearly, for the traveller’s dates, and put the agreed arrangements in writing.

The family-planning guide turns this into questions for airlines and properties.
