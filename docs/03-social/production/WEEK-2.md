## SOC-003 — Jeddah-তে নামবেন, Madinah থেকে ফিরবেন—তুলনাটা কীভাবে?

- **Format / language:** REEL / bn
- **Source:** [Dhaka–Jeddah/Madinah Flights: Open-Jaw, Baggage & Quote Checks (2026)](https://uraltravel.co.uk/blog/dhaka-to-jeddah-madinah-open-jaw-flight-strategy-biman-saudia) — Which route shape fits; Compare flight quotes on the same basis
- **Decision / evidence:** Compare both open-jaw directions and same-city roundtrip using the same dates and whole-trip costs. (SF-03; STRONG at the stated scope.)
- **Status / gate:** DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK; G-01
- **Funnel / KPI:** CONSIDERATION; Watch retention; route-comparison questions; qualified guide visits
- **Visual direction:** Simple DAC/JED/MED route arrows with no airline logo or price; small label: পরিকল্পনার উদাহরণ, live schedule নয়.
- **Do not claim:** No nonstop/daily availability, exact fare, blanket baggage or saving.

### Spoken script

**Target:** roughly 35–50 seconds including the hook; time an actual Bengali read and trim pauses/repetition rather than speed-reading.

Jeddah-তে নামবেন, Madinah থেকে ফিরবেন—তুলনাটা কীভাবে?

Umrah flight তুলনায় শুধু ticket price দেখলে একটা খরচ বাদ পড়ে যেতে পারে—মাটির পথে যাতায়াত। একই date-এ তিনটি option দেখুন: Jeddah গিয়ে সেখান থেকেই ফেরা, Jeddah গিয়ে Madinah থেকে ফেরা, আর Madinah গিয়ে Jeddah থেকে ফেরা। যেটা আপনার trip-এ প্রযোজ্য, তার airport transfer ও দুই শহরের যাতায়াত যোগ করুন। যাত্রী, baggage আর change terms-ও এক রাখুন। Open-jaw সুবিধাজনক হতে পারে; কিন্তু সবসময় সস্তা নয়।

### Shot / text plan

- 0–3s: route question.
- 3–18s: three schematic arrows; label them comparison options, not operating flights.
- 18–35s: add transfer + baggage + change terms to each option.
- Final: Compare total + workload; guide end-card.

Use the spoken wording for subtitles. Proofread auto-captions manually. The cut is a draft until the producer checks pacing.

## SOC-005 — An affiliate click is not a booking. A booking is not a payout.

- **Format / language:** LINKEDIN TEXT / en
- **Source:** [Can You Really Make Money With Travel Affiliate Marketing? I'm Building Ural Travel to Find Out](https://uraltravel.co.uk/blog/can-you-make-money-travel-affiliate-marketing-ural-experiment) — Why a Travel Affiliate Click Does Not Always Earn a Commission
- **Decision / evidence:** Define commercial stages honestly before judging a publishing experiment. (SF-13; EXPERIMENTAL at the stated scope.)
- **Status / gate:** DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK; G-01
- **Funnel / KPI:** TRUST; Relevant professional replies; qualified article visits; creator interest recorded separately
- **Visual direction:** Text-only. Optional plain funnel diagram if already available; no invented analytics screenshot.
- **Do not claim:** No current URAL earnings, referral reward amount, network benchmark or measured conversion claim.

### LinkedIn copy

An affiliate click is not a booking. A booking is not a payout.

That distinction matters when evaluating a travel-content project. A reader can find a guide useful without buying anything. A partner click can lead to an unavailable product. A booking can remain pending or be cancelled. An approved commission can still be waiting for payout.

A useful scorecard therefore keeps the stages separate:
• Did the content help someone make a planning decision?
• Did they reach a relevant partner?
• Did a qualifying booking follow?
• Was commission approved—and eventually paid?

URAL’s published affiliate experiment discusses this uncertainty openly. It is a framework for learning, not an income promise or a new financial-results announcement.

The article contains a clearly labelled creator referral. Creator interest belongs in its own reporting lane, separate from traveller booking enquiries.

The URAL experiment explains the distinction and its limitations.
