## SOC-014 — Haram-এর কাছে Hotel। কিন্তু বাবা-মা কোন পথে যাবেন?

- **Format / language:** REEL / bn

- **Decision / evidence:** Ask for the exact entrance, lift, room and return route rather than accepting an area label. (SF-04; USEFUL SECONDARY at the stated scope.)
- **Status / gate:** DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK; G-01
- **Funnel / KPI:** PLANNING; Watch retention; saves; hotel-access planning questions

## SOC-015 — An account is a digital state. A confirmed travel task is an operational state.

- **Format / language:** LINKEDIN TEXT / en
- **Source:** [Nusuk & Saudi Visa Bio for Bangladeshis: Registration, Biometrics and Rawdah Permits](https://uraltravel.co.uk/blog/nusuk-app-saudi-visa-bio-guide-bangladesh-rawdah-permit) — Nusuk service distinctions; booking confirmation; access on actual phone/network
- **Decision / evidence:** Explain why account creation, approval and usable confirmation need separate language. (SF-13; EXPERIMENTAL at the stated scope.)
- **Status / gate:** DRAFT — PRODUCTION / SOURCE CHECK; G-01
- **Funnel / KPI:** TRUST; Substantive product/industry replies; relevant guide visits
- **Visual direction:** Text-only; optional simple state labels, never a fabricated logged-in app screenshot.
- **Do not claim:** No claim that current URAL form/alert defects have been fixed or an app flow personally tested.

### LinkedIn copy

An account is a digital state. A confirmed travel task is an operational state.

Travel interfaces often place several steps close together: registration, application, payment, approval, booking and confirmation. Readers can reasonably assume that completing one means the next is finished too.

Content should make the boundaries visible. In an Umrah planning journey, a visa application, a Nusuk account and a specific appointment are different tasks. A guide should explain which service is being used, what has actually been confirmed and what the traveller still needs to check.

This is also a useful product-writing question: does the success message describe the action the system really completed?

URAL’s Nusuk guide is based on published instructions, not a test of someone’s authenticated account. Keeping that evidence boundary visible is part of useful travel intelligence.

The Nusuk guide uses this distinction to organize its reader tasks.
