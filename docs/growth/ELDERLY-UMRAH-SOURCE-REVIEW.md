## Pre-edit audit and brief

Audience: Bangladeshi families travelling for Umrah with an older parent or relative who is frail, uses a manual
or powered wheelchair, or cannot walk long distances. One job: separate what the airline can actually arrange, what
the mosque authority publishes about on-site mobility, and what only the clinician, the property and the ground
staff can answer. Demand, rankings and conversion are **not measured**; the article order is editorial.

Three data locations, App overrides, SEO copy, schema and renderer were read. The existing EN body was five short
numbered sections with three FAQ pairs and **three generic homepage references** (`saudia.com`, `haj.gov.sa`,
`gacr.gov.sa`). BN mirrored it with the same homepages and four links. The 16 October "checked against official
guidelines" sentence therefore certified nothing specific. The third domain, `gacr.gov.sa`, **failed to resolve on
2026-10-19**, and the authority's actual public site is `alharamain.gov.sa`; the authority-specific claim rests on
the wrong domain.

The body also carried blanket promises: free service "across major scheduled carriers departing DAC", free personal
wheelchair carriage as a general airline rule, return "at the aircraft door", a fixed first-floor/mezzanine circuit,
single- and two-seat scooters steered by a companion, flat zero-incline hotel streets, a northern-only women's
cluster in Madinah, a 20-day medicine supply and an insulin cold-chain instruction. Several are plausible but were
not sourced. Others contradict what the operators currently publish.
