# Zamzam / dates / gold customs — source and claim review

Pre-edit audit written **2026-10-19, Asia/Dhaka**. One EN/BN pair:
`official-zamzam-water-dates-gold-customs-rules-jeddah-dhaka-airport` (`blog-21`). Not legal advice, not a customs
ruling, and not a claim that any officer will assess your bag the way this page describes.

## The two findings that drive this revision

**1. The article quotes the wrong Zamzam price, and an earlier guard enforces it.** The pair says one sealed 5-litre
box costs **48 SAR (~BDT 620)**. Tibah Airports, the operator of Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport
in Madinah, publishes **SAR 12.50** for the 5-litre pilgrim bottle. The 48 SAR figure belongs to a different product:
a 24 × 330 ml carton sold through the Nusuk app for home delivery inside the Kingdom (reported June 2025). An earlier
correction wave conflated the two and wrote the home-delivery price onto the airport bottle — and added a build guard
that fails if the older range reappears. That guard has to be rewritten, not worked around.

**2. "Bangladesh permits 5–10 kg of dates per passenger duty-free" has no source.** No passenger allowance for dates
in kilograms exists in the material that could be opened. What exists is a general rule: personal goods are exempt
unless the quantity is considered commercial, and commercial quantities are seized. The pair also quotes date prices
per kilogram and a "40%" saving — neither is traceable to a dated source.
