# Ramadan / I'tikaf — source and claim review

Pre-edit audit written **2026-10-19, Asia/Dhaka**. One EN/BN article pair:
`ramadan-umrah-itikaf-guide-bangladesh-booking-budget` (`blog-19`). Not a fatwa, not a booking agent, not a
prediction of the moon, and not a claim that any particular pilgrim will be given an I'tikaf place.

## Pre-edit audit and brief

Audience: a Bangladeshi household deciding whether to travel for Ramadan 1448 (February–March 2027), whether to
register for I'tikaf in the Two Holy Mosques, and how much of the trip to commit before quotes exist. One job: give
the calendar and the Ministry/Authority deadlines accurately, then be honest about what an official I'tikaf place is
and who has actually been eligible for one.

The existing pair is six sections with no FAQ section (so no FAQ schema), four internal links per locale and six
references. Its calendar arithmetic is right. Its most consequential weakness is that it treats official I'tikaf
registration as something a Bangladeshi pilgrim on an Umrah visa can expect to use; the Authority's published
conditions say permits have been for citizens and residents holding a valid residency permit. It also describes the
1448 season window vaguely and states a Haramain journey time and booking channel it does not source.

## Execution outcome

_(appended after implementation and verification)_

### Execution outcome — 19 October 2026

The EN body, BN mirror, both metadata blocks, home-page snippets, SERP copy and FAQ configuration were rewritten
against the eight opened pages. Slug, author and 27 September 2026 publication date are unchanged; `updatedAt` is
`2026-10-19` in both locales. No new URL was created.

**The material correction.** The old pair treated official I'tikaf registration as something any pilgrim could use.
The Authority's own 2025 service description confines permits to applicants aged 18+ who are "citizens or residents
in the Kingdom holding a valid residency permit", free of charge, with an awareness brief in Arabic, English and Urdu,
and the 2026 announcement routes identity through Nafath for citizens and residents without publishing a visitor
route. The article now says plainly that a reader on an Umrah visa should not plan around an official I'tikaf place,
may still pray in the Haram, and should confirm the 1448 conditions with the Authority.

**What was added.** The Ministry's four dated cut-offs now replace the vague "season window": visa issuance ends
1 Shawwal (9 March 2027), entry ends 15 Shawwal (23 March 2027), departure is required by 30 Shawwal (7 April 2027),
and the season opened on 31 May 2026 with Nusuk permits from 1 June. The article states that 1448 I'tikaf registration
has not been announced, and explains the real lead time — the 1447 notice appeared one day before opening. It
separates the Nusuk Umrah permit from the I'tikaf permit, and it corrects the Madinah-first routing so the Ihram is
entered at Dhul Hulayfah before travel rather than performing Umrah before reaching Makkah.

**What was removed.** The "about two and a half hours" Haramain journey time and the claim that tickets are "booked
only through" the operator's portal, plus the unqualified "free" and "online-only" framing (now attributed to the
2025 announcement). The BDT tables stay removed; the DIY baseline remains labelled as a non-Ramadan, dated shape.

**Structure.** Seven question-led sections and a contiguous six-question FAQ (section 8), five point-of-action
cautions — season cut-offs, eligibility, annual announcement, budget basis, route/visa — six curated links per locale
(hub, Ihram, Nusuk, hotel/rail, DIY, food), eight specific references replacing six, a qualified optional eSIM CTA,
and suppression of the generic price panel for this slug. No HowTo: the registration portal is identity-gated and was
not observed, so no procedure is published.
