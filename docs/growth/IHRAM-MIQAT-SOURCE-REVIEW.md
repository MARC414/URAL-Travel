# Ihram / Miqat — source/claim review

Pre-edit audit and claim ledger written **2026-10-19, Asia/Dhaka** (Saudi site clocks showed 18 October 2026 UTC).
Existing EN/BN slug: `wearing-ihram-dhaka-airport-vs-transit-flight-miqat-rules`. One article pair. It is **not** a
personal fatwa, a school-specific ruling, a flight-tracking service or an inspected airport/rail procedure.

## Pre-edit audit and brief

Audience: Bangladeshi pilgrims flying Dhaka–Jeddah (direct or via a Gulf hub) or Dhaka–Madinah first, who need to
know what the rule requires before landing and what to do on their actual route. One job: separate the **published
boundary rule** from **route-specific practice** and from **personal religious rulings**. Demand and rankings are
unmeasured; the section order is editorial.

The existing pair was four short sections with three FAQs and **three homepage references** (`haj.gov.sa`,
`gaca.gov.sa`, `nusuk.sa`). It made several precise operational claims that no opened page supports, and it assigned
specific miqats to specific flight corridors. Because this is a religious-obligation article, an unsupported
instruction is worse than an admitted gap: a reader may act on it.

The generic price panel is already not rendered for this slug by category rules; no change needed there.

## References opened (2026-10-19)

1. `https://haj.gov.sa/en/Umrah/Miqaats` — the rule, the Jeddah-airport statement, the five points and distances,
   the return/Fidyah sequence, and the Ibn Abbas narration.
2. `https://haj.gov.sa/-/media/Project/HAJJ/PDF-and-FILES/Awareness-Guides/Ihram-Guide/English/en-ihram-guide.pdf`
   — Ihram begins with intention and Talbiyah; ghusl and fragrance recommended; garments by sex; the Talbiyah text;
   the air/land/sea requirement; the "airlines usually announce" statement; the three categories of people;
   prohibitions. **Retrieval note:** the PDF's text layer is partial (some layout blocks and images do not extract),
   so only the quoted sentences are used.
3. `https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1526` — Ibn Abbas: the Prophet fixed Dhul Hulaifa, Al-Juhfa, Qarn al-Manazil and
   Yalamlam, and these are for those who pass them intending Hajj or Umrah.
4. `https://umrah.nusuk.sa/Journey` — Ihram as step 1 of the Umrah sequence (intention, Talbiyah, garments).
5. `https://hajj.nusuk.sa/nusuk/hajj-rituals` — "When the pilgrim is on a plane approaching the designated Miqat point
   by air, they must make their intention for Ihram"; recommended pre-Ihram acts.
6. `https://aliftaa.jo/research-fatwa-english/2982/A-Pilgrim-Traveling-by-Plane-Assumes-Ihram-upon-Reaching-the-Miqat`
   — Jordan Iftaa fatwa 2982 (29 September 2014): Jeddah is within the miqat, but wearing the garments at home or on
   the plane and making the intention at the place parallel to the miqat is permissible. Attributed example of a
   scholarly position, not Saudi visa/entry law and not a personal ruling.

**Retrieval failures / limits:** no carrier page confirming any specific airline's announcement practice was found;
GACA's role in the earlier reference could not be substantiated and GACA is no longer cited. No airport page was
opened for prayer-room or changing facilities. No rail page was opened for on-board miqat announcements.

## Implementation scope (recorded before edits)

Five question-led sections and a contiguous six-question FAQ. **No HowTo**: the visible sequence is a religious act
(intention, Talbiyah), and the standard forbids publishing personal religious instruction as an administrative
checklist. Five immediate accessible cautions (`ihramPoint`, `ihramGarments`, `ihramTransit`, `ihramMadinah`,
`ihramPersonal`) placed at the decision each one governs. Six specific references replace three homepages; five
curated internal links per locale (women's Ihram guide, main DIY, Nusuk/Rawdah, hotel/rail or flights, `/umrah`).
Qualified optional transfer CTA. No new URL; no price, no ruling, no guaranteed announcement. Preserve publication
date and author; date the revision truthfully. No merge or deployment.

### Execution outcome — 19 October 2026

The EN body, the BN mirror, both metadata blocks, the home-page snippets, the SERP copy and the FAQ heading config
were rewritten against the six opened pages above. No new URL was created; the slug, author and 27 September 2026
publication date are unchanged, and `updatedAt` is `2026-10-19` in both locales.

**What changed.** The three homepages were replaced by six specific pages, each with a Bengali label. The rule is
quoted from the Ministry rather than paraphrased: air arrivals "must not bypass the Miqat (or the point parallel to
it) without entering the state of Ihram", and Jeddah airport "is neither a Miqat nor a place for entering Ihram".
The five points now carry the Ministry's own distances (420, 187/204, 94, 115 and 92 km) instead of an unverified
"11 km from Madinah". Ihram is defined as intention plus Talbiyah, with the Ministry's formula and the Nusuk step
order, so the garments are visibly preparation rather than the state. The crossing remedy now follows the Ministry's
sequence — return to the Miqat; Fidyah if Ihram was assumed after passing — and the "Dam" paraphrase is gone.

**What was removed.** The "30 to 45 minutes before touchdown" figure, the trilingual crew script, the airline-by-airline
and corridor-by-corridor miqat table, the "11 km outside Madinah" distance, the 7 kg cabin figure, the Ashkona camp
and Terminal 1/2 room specifics, the guaranteed transit changing facilities and the on-board Haramain-rail intention
instruction. In their place: the Ministry's statement that airlines "usually announce" when the aircraft is aligned,
an explicit "the announcement is not guaranteed" line, a route-neutral explanation of the parallel point, and a
statement that no airport or rail announcement was verified.

**What was added.** The three Ministry categories (in Makkah, between, beyond), the precaution of consulting a scholar before entering Ihram
when the case is unclear (the guide's conditional-intention formula is named in the ledger but not reproduced), women's clothing and the niqab/gloves prohibition
as the guide states them, the Talbiyah end point for the Mu'tamir, and the Dhat 'Irq/Umar context in the references.
A qualified optional transfer CTA replaces the unqualified Terminal 1 collect-and-drive promise, and the generic
price/TravelIntelligence panel is now explicitly excluded for this slug.
