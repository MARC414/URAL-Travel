# Food guide — source review and claim ledger

**Asset:** `bangladeshi-halal-food-guide-makkah-madinah-budget-meals` (blog-22), EN + BN.
**Review date:** 19 October 2026. **Reviewer:** agent-owned public-source review; no owner-supplied material.
**Method:** every quantitative or locational claim in the published pair was listed, then checked against a source that
could actually be opened on the review date. A claim that could not be checked was removed or rewritten as a method the
reader performs, not replaced with a different invented figure.

## Retrieval limits

- The Balady **news** pages on inspection coverage returned HTTP 500 on the review date, so no coverage percentage is
  quoted. The health-certificate service page (7) opened and is cited instead.
- No restaurant was visited, no menu was photographed, no meal was bought. **Every price claim is therefore absent, not
  estimated.** The guide tells the reader what to ask and what to compare.
- Delivery-app coverage (HungerStation, Jahez) and hotel room-cooking rules were not verified; the copy sends those to
  the app and the hotel.
- Restaurant names in Misfalah and the Madinah central blocks change with tenancy; the copy deliberately names none.
