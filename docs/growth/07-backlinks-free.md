# 07 — Free Backlink & Off-Site Authority Methods

**Perspective:** external authority — the missing ingredient (root cause D in [01](01-indexing-crawling.md)). No budget required.
**Prereqs:** [01](01-indexing-crawling.md) (pages must be indexable) + [03](03-content-topical-authority.md) (pages must be worth linking to). **Do not start this until pages are live, indexed, and genuinely useful** — links to thin pages are wasted.
**Feeds into:** [05](05-aeo-geo.md) (mentions feed answer engines too).

---

## 1. The mindset

A new travel-affiliate site has zero authority. You need **relevant, earned** links — quality over quantity. Ten links from real Bangladeshi/travel sources beat a thousand spam directory links (which can trigger penalties). **Never buy links, never use link farms/PBNs, never spam comments.** Every method below is free and legitimate.

---

## 2. Foundation links (do these first — free, fast, safe)

These establish your entity and are expected of any real business:
- **Business/brand profiles:** Google Business Profile (if eligible), and consistent profiles on relevant platforms. Same name, same URL everywhere (NAP consistency — helps [05](05-aeo-geo.md)).
- **Social profiles** linking to the site: create and complete profiles (Facebook Page — huge in Bangladesh — plus Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube, X, LinkedIn). Add these as `sameAs` in `Organization` schema ([05](05-aeo-geo.md) §4).
- **Free web properties** you control: a Medium publication, a Substack, a Blogger, that repost/summarize your best guides with a canonical or link back.

---

## 3. Content-driven links (the real growth engine)

Earn links by publishing things people *want* to cite:
- **The "visa-free countries for Bangladeshi passport" pillar** ([03](03-content-topical-authority.md) Cluster A, [04](04-keywords.md) §4) is your #1 link magnet — it's factual, shareable, and updated yearly. Promote it hard.
- **Data/roundup posts:** "Cost of Umrah from Bangladesh 2026," "Cheapest months to fly Dhaka→[destinations]." Journalists and bloggers cite concrete numbers.
- **Original comparisons and checklists** (downloadable visa document checklists, packing lists) attract links from forums and community posts.

---

## 4. Outreach & community (free, high-relevance)

- **Bangladeshi travel communities:** Facebook groups (Travelers of Bangladesh, ঘুরে বেড়াই, etc.), forums, and subreddits (r/bangladesh, r/travel). **Participate genuinely and link only when it truly answers the question** — self-promo spam gets you banned and does nothing. This also feeds AEO ([05](05-aeo-geo.md) §5).
- **Quora & Reddit answers:** answer real visa/cost/flight questions thoroughly, link to your relevant guide as the source. LLMs ingest these too.
- **Guest posts / expert quotes:** offer to write for Bangladeshi lifestyle/travel blogs or provide a quote (HARO-style services, some free) as "a Bangladesh travel resource."
- **Broken-link building:** find dead links on travel resource pages, suggest your live page as a replacement. Free, high success rate.
- **Unlinked mentions:** if anyone mentions "URAL travel" without linking, politely ask for the link.

---

## 5. Local & niche directories (selective — quality only)

- Reputable **Bangladesh business directories** and **travel-specific directories** only. Skip generic global "submit your site" farms — they're worthless or harmful.
- **Tourism boards / embassy resource pages** — long shot, but a link from an authoritative `.gov`/tourism site is gold; worth a polite outreach for your factual visa guides.

---

## 6. Internal reinforcement (don't forget)

Your own [internal linking](02-internal-linking.md) is the cheapest "backlink" of all — it's fully in your control and directly fixes indexing. **Max out internal linking (02) before chasing external links.** External authority amplifies a well-linked site; it can't rescue an orphaned one.

---

## 7. What to avoid (penalty risk — high)

- Paid links, link exchanges at scale, PBNs, comment/forum spam, automated directory blasts, hidden links, irrelevant links. Google's link spam systems devalue or penalize these. **A tool must never do these**; if the owner requests them, flag the risk.

---

## 8. Verification & success metric

- [ ] `sameAs` social profiles live and in `Organization` schema.
- [ ] Link-magnet pillar published and promoted in ≥ 3 communities (genuinely).
- [ ] Track referring domains in GSC → Links report.
- **Success:** ≥ 10 quality referring domains within 2–3 months; branded search and referral traffic rising. Growth here compounds slowly — be patient and never cut corners into spam.
