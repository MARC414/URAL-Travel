## 4. Claim-level ledger

**Reading convention:** status applies to the claim in the second column. “Safe wording/action” is an evidence-bounded instruction for a later brief, not approved article prose. Each row inherits **checked 2026-10-16** and its linked source's effective/version date from §3. `EN+BN` means both current surfaces or required parity when filling a documented gap; a locale-only claim is marked. Source IDs supply owner and exact URL; location labels supply the potential section without choosing headings/components.

- `VERIFIED`: the stated scoped fact was read in primary evidence (or directly observed in repository code for R rows).
- `QUALIFIED`: support exists only with the listed conditions; the qualification must accompany any use.
- `CONFLICT`: published wording contradicts primary evidence, another material surface, or the rendering contract. Remains open for owner disposition even where a source-backed correction is proposed.
- `UNVERIFIED`: required evidence/current applicability not established; do not assert.
- `DROP`: remove the unsupported/generalized claim rather than cosmetically hedge it, subject to owner approval before editing.
