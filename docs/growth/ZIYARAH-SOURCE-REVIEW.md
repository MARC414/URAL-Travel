# Ziyarah pair — source review and claim ledger

**Asset:** `makkah-madinah-badr-taif-historical-ziyarah-taxi-guide` (blog-23), EN + BN.
**Review date:** 19 October 2026. **Reviewer:** agent-owned public-source review; no owner-supplied material.
**Method:** every quantitative or locational claim in the published pair was listed, then checked against a page that
could actually be opened on the review date. A claim that could not be checked was removed — not swapped for a
different invented number. Where two official pages disagree, both values are published with the disagreement named so
the reader knows what to re-check on the day.

The pair is a **site-and-transport planner**, not a ritual manual. No section tells a pilgrim how to perform a
visitation, what to say at a grave, or what a visit earns. The Quba hadith is quoted because the article's own heading
claims it, and it is quoted with its grading.

## Retrieval limits

- **No transport price survived.** No authority, operator or app publishes a Ziyarah car tariff, so the pair now
  contains no SAR or BDT figure for a car in Makkah, Madinah, Taif or Badr. This is a deliberate absence, not an
  oversight; the copy teaches the reader what to fix in writing before the car moves.
- The Hira opening hours are **genuinely disputed between official pages** [1][2][3]. The pair prints all three and
  tells the reader to check the district site on the day.
- The cable-car fare and the Monday closure come from secondary listings; the operator's own SPA releases [10] give
  capacity and duration only. Published as "about SAR 100, confirm locally".
- rcmc.gov.sa, Nusuk and the Madinah development authority were **not** used as sources for this pair; nothing in the
  copy is attributed to them any more.
- csmadinah.com showed "temporarily closed" stop badges on the review date [9]. The reason is not published, so the
  copy reports the badge and does not speculate.
- No site was visited, no car was hired, no ticket was bought. Numbers here are what the cited page says today.
