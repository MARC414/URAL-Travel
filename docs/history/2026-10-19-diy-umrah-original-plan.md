## What you need to check for the Nusuk/biometrics article

The separate source ledger is `NUSUK-BIOMETRICS-AUDIT.md`. The editor can research and correct text now;
the following require a real device/account or official support. **Do not create fake visa applications or reserve trial permits.**

1. **Current visitor registration:** on your own device or with a consenting eligible traveller, record date,
   Android/iOS, OS and app version, account type and general visa category. Note actual field labels and OTP delivery
   channel/format. No need to record the OTP, email, phone number, passport or visa number. If you do not have a valid
   visa/account, mark the authenticated flow **not tested**; do not fabricate one.
2. **Application-specific biometrics:** ask the authorized visa provider or official application support whether
   Saudi Visa Bio is requested for **your exact application** and what the official alternative is after repeated
   capture failure. The Ministry listing is not proof that every tourist/stopover traveller needs it. Do not attempt
   skin-treatment “hacks.” No fingerprints, face images or passport scans should be sent to URAL/chat.
3. **Rawdah workflow:** if already legitimately booking, note whether attendance confirmation appears, the displayed
   confirmation deadline and when the entry code is available. Record any repeat-visit or Instant Track eligibility
   message exactly, with date. Do not consume a slot just to test, spoof GPS or share a usable QR/barcode.
4. **Network/permit display:** test that your own app opens and shows the issued permit on the actual phone/network.
   Record the operator or travel-eSIM provider and whether the connection works; do not conclude that all travel
   eSIMs receive Saudi-operator zero-rated Nusuk access. Ask official support if the display requirement is unclear.
   We have not established a universal screenshot-rejection policy.
5. **Language and usability:** check that both pages make the difference between visa application, Nusuk account,
   Umrah permit and Rawdah appointment clear. Buying an eSIM must not be described as a permit requirement.

Safe evidence: a written description with date/app version or a fully redacted screenshot of labels/error text.
Mask names, passport/visa/application numbers, contact details, OTPs, faces and all QR/barcodes. Never send credentials.
