**What it answers (verbatim-backed):**

- **Scope note (matters for the spine):** entry-requirements page states it "is for people
  travelling on a full **'British citizen' passport** from the UK." → FCDO does **not** define
  the rules for a BD passport + BRP holder; it defers: *"If you're not sure how these
  requirements apply to you, contact the Saudi Embassy in the UK."*
- **Three entry routes for British citizens** (all checked 11 Nov 2026):
  1. **ETA** — tourism/business/study/medical/**Umrah (outside Hajj season)**; valid **730 days**,
     multiple entries, **180 days total** in that period; apply at `ksavisa.sa` before travel;
     **no health insurance included**; separate Hajj visa required for Hajj.
  2. **e-visa** — **365 days**, multiple entry, **90 days total**; `visa.visitsaudi.com` or on
     arrival; **includes health insurance up to SAR 100,000** (FCDO advises extra cover beyond
     that); separate Hajj visa required.
  3. **Electronic visa waiver** — single entry, up to **180 days**; no insurance; `visa.mofa.gov.sa`;
     at least **48 hours before the flight** (or up to 90 days ahead); flight details required.
- **Passport validity:** expiry ≥ **6 months** after arrival date.
- **Hajj-season flight restriction:** Saudi authorities have stopped non-Hajj visitors flying to
  **Jeddah, Madinah and Taif** around Hajj — a Hajj visa may be needed **to board**; other
  airports still usable for entry.
- **Vaccines:** meningococcal quadrivalent (ACWY) certificate for Hajj/Umrah pilgrims; yellow
  fever if arriving from a transmission-risk country.
- **Pilgrimage page:** Hajj places **only** via the official **Nusuk Hajj** platform; book an
  **ATOL-protected** operator for Umrah; ETD if passport lost during Hajj costs **£125**;
  British Consulate General Jeddah **+966 11 481 9100**.
- **⚠ Live risk frame (the article's "practical outlook" spine, dated):** FCDO advises **against
  all travel within 10km of the Yemen border** and **against all but essential travel to the rest
  of Saudi Arabia**; King Khalid International Airport was **struck 10 October 2026** and airports
  "continue to be targeted"; Houthi missiles/drones since **13 July**; *"Your travel insurance
  could be invalidated if you travel against advice."* Current at 11 Nov 2026.
