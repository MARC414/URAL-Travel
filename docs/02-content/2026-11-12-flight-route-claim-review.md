# Flight hub and route claim review — 12 November 2026

Scope: the nine existing routes, English and Bengali, including saved HTML and
metadata. This is not a live airline schedule, fare quote, eligibility decision,
checkout test or a verification of every linked destination guide.

- [Nepal Department of Immigration visa information](https://immigration.gov.np/visa-information) describes a tourist visa on arrival and a gratis provision for SAARC citizens (except Afghanistan), up to 30 days on the first visit in a visa year. Verify applicability and current procedures; approval is not guaranteed. An older `/en/page/tourist-visa` URL returned a 404 during this review.
- [Thailand's official e-Visa portal](https://www.thaievisa.go.th/) explains its online process and says applications are subject to approval. Check the current Dhaka mission and individual application requirements.
- [Singapore ICA's SG Arrival Card guidance](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card-with-electronic-health-declaration), last updated 28 October 2026, distinguishes the declaration from a visa and states exceptions and timing. The flight page points readers to ICA rather than making a universal boarding claim.
- [US Department of State visitor visa guidance](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) provides the official visitor-visa starting point. Individual eligibility, interview and transit requirements vary.
- Saudi foreign-document exceptions and the limitations of available evidence are recorded in [the Saudi source review](2026-11-12-saudi-visa-source-review.md). The flight guide does not promise visa on arrival or categorically exclude every qualifying foreign-document route.

The former route copy included fixed booking windows, airline frequency, flight
lengths, baggage quantities, processing times and universal entry-document
requirements without a current route-specific source. Those assertions have been
removed or replaced by checks of the current itinerary and official rules. Listed
airlines are historical candidates, not a promise of current service or nonstop
operation. No fixed fare is published. Bangkok search defaults to BKK, with DMK
available for the traveller to select. For Los Angeles and the Kathmandu return,
no destination-specific internal visa/hotel guide exists; the UI labels broad
hubs honestly, and Los Angeles also links to the official US guidance.
