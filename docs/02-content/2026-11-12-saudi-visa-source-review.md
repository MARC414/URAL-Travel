Scope: the Bangladeshi-reader Saudi visa guide and related Umrah/stopover copy. This is a source review, not a test application, boarding check, personalized eligibility decision, or a review of every rule on every linked page.

| [Saudia transit page](https://www.saudia.com/transit-visa) | Previously reviewed in October 2026. | Reopening returned a 404 on 12 November 2026. Detailed airline-specific procedures, refund conditions and acceptance are not republished as verified current facts. |
| [Saudi eVisa portal terms](https://visa.visitsaudi.com/Home/TermsConditions) | The portal's terms say submitted applications cannot be cancelled or refunded and unsuccessful applicants cannot recover the application fee. | Those terms govern that eVisa service. They do not establish the refund policy of Package Visa flights, hotels, provider services or an airline stopover. No blanket package refund rule is published here. |

Keep source links and dates in the published guidance. Do not infer a universal restriction from a provider-specific statement or a universal entitlement from a general marketing FAQ. If an exact rule or provider term cannot be verified, omit the unsupported claim rather than inventing it.
